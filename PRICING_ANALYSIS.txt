      Mitigation: 4-hr per-session cap + credit checks eliminate this scenario.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. MULTI-ACCOUNT CREDIT FARMING [MITIGATED]
     Risk: Users create multiple free accounts to farm 5 starter credits each.
     Max loss per fake account: $1.05 (5 credits × $0.21).
     Mitigations applied:
       a) email must be non-empty in JWT — blocks anonymous/no-email signups
       b) email_verified = true required — extracted from Clerk JWT; blocks
          users who signed up with an unverified disposable email
       c) DB partial unique index on credit_transactions (user_id) WHERE
          reason = 'starter_grant' — migration 005_starter_grant_unique.sql
       d) Code try/except catches any race that slips past the app-level check

     Remaining gap: CAPTCHA on signup (handled by Clerk config, not code).
     Add Clerk's Bot Protection in the Clerk dashboard for full coverage.

  2. IP-BASED RATE LIMITING BYPASS [FIXED]
     Risk: X-Forwarded-For header can be spoofed to bypass per-IP rate limits.
     Fix: rate_limit.py now uses a hybrid key function:
       - Authenticated requests → keyed on user_id (extracted from JWT sub claim)
       - Anonymous requests → keyed on remote IP (fallback)
     Result: Spoofing the IP header has zero effect for logged-in users.

  3. RACE CONDITION IN STARTER GRANT [FIXED]
     Risk: Two simultaneous /credits/balance calls could double-grant credits.
     Fix: maybe_grant_starter() now wraps the INSERT in try/except; the DB
     partial unique index (item 1c above) ensures only one succeeds.

  4. REFUND USES WRONG AMOUNT [FIXED]
     Bug: refund_credit() always refunded 1 credit regardless of lecture cost.
     Fix: now fetches total_duration_seconds from the lecture row and calls
     credits_for_duration(duration) to refund the exact amount deducted.

  5. PRO PLAN NO-SESSION-CAP [FIXED]
     Previously live_max_duration_seconds = None (unlimited).
     Now fixed to 14,400s (4 hrs) to prevent unbounded API calls in one session.

    At 1-hr lectures: 7 lectures/month = 7 hrs of AI-assisted study.
    Compared to buying credits separately: 15 cr at $0.499/cr = $7.49 value.
    Plus unlimited sessions (within credit limit), PDF export, Q&A, sharing.
    Equivalent commercial AI tools: $20-40/month. We're at $9.99. FAIR.

    Gets 30 credits + 4-hr session cap + 40-hr/month ceiling.
    Heavy user buying 80 credits/month separately: 2 × 60cr power packs = $39.98.
    With Pro plan: $19.99 plan gives 30 credits, then 1 power pack ($19.99) for 50 more = $39.98 total.
    Same cost, but Pro plan also unlocks all premium features.
    FAIR — customer pays same or less than pure credit purchases.

    No subscription, no commitment.

    Compare: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai charge $8-18/month for just transcription.
    We offer full AI analysis (summary, Q&A, PDF, sharing) per lecture. FAIR.
